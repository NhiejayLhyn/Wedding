Our Story

A Love Story

Where It All Began

This story sparked from one typical, "Hello, Ma'am" in 2022 which slowly flourished into a long distance friendship until the onset of Brigada Eskwela 2023 when he asked, "Ano bang kailangan mo sa classroom?"

That simple one liner unfolded a love story that thrived across miles between Niejhay and Lhyn, which led to this very special celebration of two different individuals, finding their way to becoming one.

The Journey

Being miles apart from each other, Niejhay and Lhyn proved the saying "Absence makes the heart grow fonder" as they faced the days that turned into years without being physically together.

Instead of holding each other's hand, they held onto phones that kept their hearts connected. They celebrated milestones, birthdays, and anniversaries through screens, comforted each other through calls, and shared their dreams through late-night conversations.

There were days when distance felt heavier than usual, yet they found ways to make it feel a little smaller. Niejhay and Lhyn's love was built not on the convenience of being together, but on patience, faith, trust, understanding, and commitment to stay in love across the miles.

Forever Starts Here

And after years of waiting, their journey now leads to the moment when "I miss you" turns to "I do."
